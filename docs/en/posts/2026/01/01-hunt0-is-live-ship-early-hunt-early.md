## What is HUNT0?

HUNT0 is a community-driven launch directory. It brings shipping and discovery into the same rhythm:

- Reserve a launch date and make shipping a real event
- Get early feedback through voting and comments
- Use leaderboards and Explore to organize “new products” by time and interest — not noise

## Launch v1.0.0: what we’ve built

v1.0.0 focuses on the core loop first: **launch → discover → vote → discuss → recap/reward**.

### 1) Home leaderboards: “what should I check out today?”

The home page is organized by time ranges so you can quickly scan what’s worth attention:

- **Top Products Launching Today**: products launching today (UTC)
- **Yesterday / This Week / This Month**: recaps with longer windows

Voting state is **synchronized in real time** across the page: vote on a product anywhere, and every instance updates immediately — no refresh needed.

### 2) Explore: filter by intent, not luck

Explore supports **category, tags (up to 10), time range, and full-text search**, with paginated results.

Under the same filters, **Premium Launch** products are prioritized above free launches in ordering — ideal for moments when visibility matters more.

### 3) Product pages: showcase, visit, and discuss in one place

Each product has a dedicated page for deeper context and community interaction:

- Key info and external links (Visit)
- Screenshot gallery and a longer “About this product”
- Reputation-weighted votes and comments
- Leaderboard/award badges (when you rank Top 3 in daily/weekly/monthly awards)

### 4) Submissions: Free queue + Premium Launch

We designed “launch” as a controlled workflow, not just posting a link:

- **Free Launch**: limited daily capacity (default **10 slots/day**) for most launches
- **Premium Launch**: paid via Stripe Checkout for launches that need stronger visibility

If a premium checkout isn’t completed, the submission stays as a draft in your dashboard and won’t appear publicly until payment succeeds.

Submissions support richer presentation:

- Up to 3 categories
- Up to 10 tags
- Contact/social links (at least one)
- Logo and screenshots for a better product page

## Reputation: make contribution count

Voting isn’t a flat “one person, one fixed vote”. HUNT0 uses **Reputation → Level → Vote Weight**:

- Earn reputation by participating (daily visits, voting, commenting, launching)
- Higher levels unlock stronger vote weight
- Leaderboards aggregate weighted votes, reflecting trusted contributors more

It’s both an incentive system and a practical way to reduce noise in early-stage communities.

## Reminders & awards: treat launches like events

To make launching feel like a real moment, we added automation around it:

- **Launch reminder email**: sent 1 hour before the UTC launch day begins
- **Daily/weekly/monthly awards**: automatically compute Top 3 and notify makers (with optional public recap)

We’ll keep iterating on discovery, leaderboards, and community incentives. Launch something, hunt something — and tell us what to improve.
